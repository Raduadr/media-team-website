Our Story

CINEVRA was built at the intersection of cinematic craft and technical problem-solving. Our background in industrial design and production engineering informs how we approach every project: with precision, adaptability, and a commitment to delivering exceptional results in any setting.

We believe sustainable, high-tech production is not a compromise; it is a competitive advantage. Our solar-powered mobile studio allows us to work responsibly while maintaining the flexibility and technical capability required for modern commercial, documentary, and remote media production.
